Smarter Procurement. Less Waiting.

A farmer should not have to spend hours waiting at a procurement centre just to find out when their turn will come. This platform is made to help farmers plan their visit to the procurement centre.

Register as a farmer Login

The problem

⏳ Hours lost waiting

Farmers reach the centre early and wait for a long time, because there is no way to know when their turn will actually come.

❓ No idea of your turn

At the centre, nobody knows how many people are ahead of them or how long the wait will be.

🚜 Crowding at peak hours

Everyone arrives at the same time, so counters are free in some hours and overcrowded in others.

💰 Unclear payment status

After procurement, farmers have to ask again and again to know the status of their payment.

How it works

1

Register

Create an account with your name, mobile number and village details.

2

Login

Sign in any time with your mobile number and password.

3

Book a slot

Choose a nearby centre and a time slot. (Coming soon in this simple version.)

4

Visit the centre

Reach the centre close to your booked time instead of standing in a long queue.
